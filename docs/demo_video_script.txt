DEMO VIDEO SCRIPT
====================

Length: about 2:22 (at 140 words per minute) to 2:40 (at 120). Narration: 258 words, plus time waiting for the screen.

BEFORE RECORDING
- Chrome, window about 1280 px wide, zoom 100-110%, bookmarks bar hidden, other tabs closed.
- Open these tabs in order:
  1. https://github.com/Zycheng114514/offgrid-tourism#the-problem
  2. https://offgrid-tourism.vercel.app/host   (forwards to our own server). Click New host so the conversation is empty.
  3. https://offgrid-tourism.vercel.app/app/
  4. https://emotions-auburn-carried-divide.trycloudflare.com/app/   switched to the Ask by SMS tab (the SMS simulator runs on our own server only)
  5. https://offgrid-tourism.vercel.app/pipeline
- Going offline: turn off Wi-Fi.
- Recording on a Mac: Shift-Command-5, record the screen or an area, Options > choose the microphone.
  Or record the screen first and add the voice afterwards.
- Right before recording, check that tabs 2 and 4 load: our own server's address changes if it restarts.
- Read the SAY lines aloud as written; they are in English because the submission must be in English.

1. Problem (24-28 s)
   SCREEN: Tab 1: stay on the "The problem" table.
   SAY: In Samosir, on Lake Toba in Indonesia, small guesthouses and food stalls are hard to find online. On OpenStreetMap, 58 percent of villages have no food or lodging listed nearby, and only 7 percent of listings have a phone number. So we built a system that works with a basic phone and a weak connection.

2. A host reports by SMS (26-30 s)
   SCREEN: Tab 2: click the example "Free text, no business name...", then Send. Wait for the question; click the suggested reply "Homestay Pelabuhan Indah"; wait for the summary; click 1, then YA. The right panel shows where each field came from.
   SAY: A host sends one SMS, in their own words. The system reads the price, the rooms and the village, and asks by SMS for anything missing. Here, it asks for the business name. The host confirms with 1, and the listing goes live.

3. Local language (8-9 s)
   SCREEN: Tab 2: click New host; click the example "Batak Toba (local language)...", then Send. The reply is in Indonesian.
   SAY: It also understands Batak Toba, the local language, and replies in Indonesian.

4. A traveller searches offline (21-24 s)
   SCREEN: Tab 3: click Download listings (it shows 20 listings, 28 KB). Turn off Wi-Fi. Type: cheap room Tomok. Switch the language menu to 中文 and click the example 便宜 住宿.
   SAY: A traveller downloads the region's listings once. That's 28 kilobytes. Then, with no connection at all, they can still search for food, rooms, boats or guides, in English, Indonesian or Chinese.

5. Ask in your own words (14-16 s)
   SCREEN: Turn Wi-Fi back on. Tab 3: switch the language back to English, open the Ask tab, type: Is there a boat from Tomok to Parapat? and click Ask. Show the answer, the listing card and the line saying who answered.
   SAY: Travellers can also ask in their own words. The server answers only from the listings, and every business it names is checked against them.

6. Ask by SMS (13-14 s)
   SCREEN: Tab 4 (Ask by SMS): click the example "Is there a boat from Tomok to Parapat?" and wait for the reply, which ends with the host's phone number.
   SAY: With phone signal but no data, the same question works by SMS, and the reply includes the host's phone number.

7. Offline assistant (17-19 s)
   SCREEN: Tab 3, Ask tab: scroll to the "Offline assistant" box and point at the button "Download offline assistant (about ... MB)". Do not click it.
   SAY: Where there is no signal at all, travellers can download a small model once, which reads questions on the phone itself. It only turns a question into a search, so it cannot invent places.

8. Honest about the demo (19-22 s)
   SCREEN: Tab 5: click the example "The model invents a name...", then step 6 "Checks against the message"; stay on the table with "rejected".
   SAY: AI is used only where rules can't do the job, and everything the model gives is checked against the original message. In this demo, the server's model outputs were prepared in advance, and all the businesses are made up.
