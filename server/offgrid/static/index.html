Offgrid Tourism

Local food and lodging information for places with weak connectivity.

Demo. All businesses, prices and phone numbers are invented; village names are real. No language model runs in this demo: model outputs for the example messages were written in advance. Messages that are not examples are handled by fixed rules and follow-up questions.

Local business side

A host with a basic phone reports what they offer by SMS, in their own language. The server reads the message, asks for anything missing, and lists the business once the host confirms.

Traveller side

A traveller downloads the region's listings while online, then searches them with no connection. With signal but no data, they can ask by SMS.
